03

Information We Collect

Section 03

The App has no sign-in and no user accounts. Jay Studio does not run its own servers for your data. Information is handled in three ways: data you enter, data processed when you use a feature, and limited technical data used by Google services (Section 08).

3.1 Data You Provide

  • Birth profiles: name, gender, date, time and place of birth (with latitude, longitude and time zone) for you and anyone you add, such as family members
  • Life context answers you choose to share in chat, such as occupation, income range or relationship status
  • Questions you type to the AI astrologers and the questions you ask for tarot readings
  • Palm photos taken with the camera or picked from your gallery
  • Preferences such as your preferred language for AI answers

3.2 Data Processed When You Use a Feature

  • AI requests: your question, the relevant birth-chart details and, for palm reading, your palm photos are sent to the Google Gemini API to generate an answer (Section 04)
  • Camera frames: analysed on your device to detect and align your hand; nothing leaves the device until you confirm a capture (Section 05)
  • Compass sensor: read on your device for the Vastu compass only (Section 07)
  • App configuration: the App downloads its settings from Google Firebase Remote Config, which uses an anonymous app-installation identifier (Section 08)

3.3 Data We Do Not Collect

Jay Studio does not collect or ask for: your email address, phone number, contacts, payment or bank details, advertising ID, precise GPS location, or any photos other than the palm photos you choose to scan. The App shows no advertisements and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.
04

AI-Generated Content & Accuracy

Section 04

The App uses Google Gemini AI to write chat answers, horoscope explanations, palm readings and tarot interpretations. Birth charts, dashas, Panchang and matching scores are calculated on your device by the App's own astronomical engine; their meaning is then explained by AI.

Every reading in this App is generated by AI. AI can make mistakes, misread a palm photo, misinterpret a chart, or state something with more confidence than it deserves.

How AI Requests Work

  • When you ask a question, request a palm reading or interpret a tarot spread, the request is sent over encrypted HTTPS to the Google Gemini API.
  • The request contains your question, the birth-chart details needed to answer it (including the profile's name and birth data), your life context answers and, for palm reading, your palm photos.
  • The answer is returned to your device and saved there. Jay Studio does not receive or store a copy.
  • Google processes these requests under its own terms and privacy policy (Section 08).

05

Camera & Palm Photos

Section 05

The App requests the CAMERA permission only for palm scanning. While the camera is open, hand detection runs entirely on your device using Google MediaPipe; the live preview is never uploaded.

How palm photos are handled:
Only the photos you confirm with "Use photo" are sent to Google Gemini to create your reading.
A copy of each confirmed palm photo is kept on your device so later scans can be checked against it, to tell whether a new scan is the same person's hand.
Palm photos are never published, sold or stored on Jay Studio servers.

Camera access is optional. You can pick a palm photo from your gallery instead; the Android photo picker gives the App access only to the photo you select. All other features work without the camera.

06

On-Device Storage

Section 06

To keep your readings available, the App saves the following in its private storage on your device, separately for each profile:

  • Birth profiles and your life context answers
  • AI chat history with each astrologer
  • Palm reports and the palm photos used for them
  • Tarot readings and their AI interpretations
  • Settings, such as language

This data stays inside the App's Android sandbox. Deleting a profile deletes its chats, palm data and tarot history. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes everything.

07

Birth Place, Location & Sensors

Section 07
  • Birth place search: when you search for a city, the name you type is looked up with your device's built-in place search (Android Geocoder), which may use Google services. The App does not request or track your current location.
  • Compass: the Vastu compass reads your phone's motion sensors on the device only. Sensor data is never saved or sent anywhere.
