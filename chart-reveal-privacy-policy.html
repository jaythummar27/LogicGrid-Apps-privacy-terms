Privacy Policy

How ChartReveal handles chart images, AI questions, app activity, purchases and notifications.

Effective and last updated: 24 October 2026 · App package: com.jaystudio.ai.chart.reveal

Know before you scan: Chart analyses, chat replies, confidence figures and any signal interpretations are AI generated, may be inaccurate, and are provided for education only. They are not financial or investment advice.

1. Who operates ChartReveal

ChartReveal is provided by Jaystudio (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the ChartReveal Android app. For questions or privacy requests, email auralinkinfotech@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

Content you choose to provide

  • One or more chart photos or screenshots selected for analysis, including multi timeframe scans.
  • Questions you type into the AI chat and the relevant analysis context needed to answer them.
  • Saved chart analyses, chat history, journal notes, collection names and app preferences.

A chart screenshot can itself contain personal or financial information. Please crop or hide account numbers, balances, names, API keys and other sensitive details before submitting it.

Technical and usage information

Depending on the feature used, the app and its service providers may process app installation identifiers, a Firebase push token, app events, diagnostic and crash data, device and app version, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad identifier and ad interactions, and purchase or subscription status. We do not require a name, phone number, brokerage login or trading account to run the chart analysis feature.

3. How AI analysis works

When you request a scan, ChartReveal sends the selected chart image or images and an analysis prompt over the internet to the Google Gemini API. Follow up chat may send your question, relevant prior analysis and, where needed, the chart image. Google processes this content to return an AI generated response. This feature does not work offline.

We do not run a separate server that stores your chart images or AI chat history for ordinary analysis. Google’s handling and retention of API inputs and outputs depends on the Gemini service tier and applicable terms; its practices can differ between paid and unpaid API use. Do not assume submitted content is private or immediately deleted. See the Gemini API terms and Google Privacy Policy.

AI output is not independently verified against a live broker feed. A chart image may be old, cropped or misread.

4. Other services and why they receive data

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google): app usage events and crash diagnostics help us understand and fix the app. We do not intentionally include chart image bytes or chat message text in custom analytics events.
  • Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Messaging (Google): configuration, free signals and push delivery use app and installation identifiers. If notifications are allowed, the device may receive signal alerts and app updates. The app also schedules some local reminders, including a daily prompt based on your device time.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: when subscriptions or in app products are available, they process product, transaction, entitlement and pseudonymous app user ID data to take payment and restore access. We do not receive your full card details through the app.
  • Google AdMob and consent tools: the current app build uses Google test ad units. The advertising SDK can process device, ad and consent information when ads are shown. If production ads are enabled in a later build, advertising providers may process identifiers and ad interactions under their own policies.
  • TradingView and market data providers: opening a live chart or market data feature can cause those providers to receive standard network and device information. Their chart data and quotes are separate from Gemini’s image analysis.
  • Optional credit checkout: if a separate credit purchase feature is made available, Firebase Authentication, a payment service and its checkout provider (which may include Razorpay) may process a pseudonymous account ID, order and payment status. The checkout will identify the payment provider before payment.

Provider policies: Firebase · RevenueCat · Google · TradingView · Razorpay.

5. Storage and retention

Saved images, analyses, chat threads, journal entries and preferences are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. You can remove individual saved items in the app, clear its data in Android settings, or uninstall it. Android backup or device transfer may copy eligible app data to your Google account or another device, depending on your device settings.

Deleting local data does not automatically remove copies already sent to Google Gemini or records held by analytics, crash, payment, advertising or market data providers. Those services keep data under their own policies and settings. We retain support emails you send us for as long as needed to answer and resolve the request, subject to applicable law.

We use HTTPS for network requests and Android app private storage for local data. No system can guarantee complete security.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Use the Android photo picker to choose only the image you want to analyze. Camera access is optional.
  • Allow or block push notifications in Android settings. Local reminders and signal alerts need notification permission on supported Android versions.
  • Review available ad privacy choices in the app or your device settings, where shown.
  • Delete saved scans, chat and journal content in the app, or clear all local app data in Android settings.
  • Manage or cancel a Google Play subscription in Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

You may have additional access, correction or deletion rights under the law where you live. Contact auralinkinfotech@gmail.com and describe your request. We may need information that lets us locate a provider record, such as a purchase ID; please do not email passwords or card numbers.

7. Android permissions

Camera lets you photograph a chart if you choose. Internet and network state support AI, charts, purchases and connected services. Notifications enable push and local alerts if granted. The app declares the Android advertising ID permission for its ad SDK. Gallery selection uses Android’s photo picker rather than broad photo library access. The app does not ask for contacts, SMS, microphone or precise location permission.

ChartReveal is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the app. Service providers may process data in countries other than yours, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards.
