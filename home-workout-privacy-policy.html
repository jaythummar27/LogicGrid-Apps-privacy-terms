Introduction

Section 01

Welcome to Home Workout Without Equipment (the "App"), developed by Varshaben Mukeshbhai Thummar ("we," "us," "our"), based in India. The App is a bodyweight fitness and workout app for Android devices.

This Privacy Policy describes what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Please read it before using the App.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall and do not use the App.

The App has no account or sign-in of any kind. This policy covers the App's local storage of your workout data, the advertising served through Google AdMob, the optional premium purchase, and the small set of Android permissions the App uses.

📊

Information the App Stores

Section 02

Everything below is created by you while using the App and is stored only on your device. We do not operate a server, and we cannot see or receive any of it.

2.1 Your Profile

  • Gender, age, height, body weight, fitness level and goal — used to size your workouts and estimate calories

2.2 Your Workout Data

  • Completed workout sessions: date, duration, exercises done, estimated calories, workout title
  • Multi-week program progress: which day of a plan you have completed
  • Body-weight log entries for the progress chart
  • Favourite exercises

All of the above is stored in a local database on your device (Android Room/SQLite). Nothing here is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

2.3 App Settings

  • Theme, language, measurement unit, sound/voice/vibration toggles, timer length and reminder time — stored locally so the App remembers your preferences

2.4 Data Collected by Advertising and Billing SDKs

The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing (see Sections 4 and 5) independently collect a small amount of device and advertising data for their own purposes. We do not receive this data ourselves.

Information We Do Not Collect

Section 03
The App does not collect or ask you for: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, an account or password of any kind, your location, camera or photo access, contacts, calendar, SMS/messages, or files.

There is no login. There is no analytics SDK of ours and no crash-reporting SDK of ours in the App. The App requests no location permission at all.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Section 04
AdMob Disclosure: The free version of the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data from your device to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This section explains how advertising works in the App.

Ad Formats Used

📱 Banner Ads A small ad shown on the home screen while you browse.
🗂️ Native Ads Ads styled to match the App, shown occasionally in exercise lists and a few other screens — never during a workout.
⚡ Interstitial Ads Full-screen ads shown occasionally at natural transition points, paced to limit how often they appear. Never shown during the workout player.
🔓 App-Open Ads Shown when you return to the App after being away for a while — not on your very first launch.
🎬 Rewarded Ads Opt-in video ads you may choose to watch to remove all ads for a limited time. Watching is never mandatory.

Data Collected by AdMob

Google AdMob independently collects the following for ad serving and measurement:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (a resettable ID you control)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • IP address (used to infer an approximate geographic location)
  • Ad interaction signals (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under its own privacy policy. We do not have access to it in an identifiable form; we see only aggregate earnings reports.

Ad Personalization & Your Choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App shows a consent form the first time it starts. You can reopen it at any time from the App's Settings screen to change or withdraw your choice.
  • On any device, you can opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Removing ads entirely: the optional premium purchase described in Section 5 turns off all advertising in the App.

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly serve child-directed advertising. Maximum ad content rating is set to General audiences.

For Google's data practices related to advertising, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Section 05

The App offers an optional paid upgrade that removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your card number, bank details or billing address.

To keep your purchase working across reinstalls and devices, purchase status may be managed by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-user identifier, your purchase/subscription status, and basic device and country information. It does not receive your name, email address or any workout data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

You can restore an existing purchase at any time using the "Restore purchase" option in the App.

Health & Fitness Information

Section 10

The App records general fitness information you provide — such as your body weight, workout duration and exercises completed — to show your own history and progress back to you, and to estimate calories burned. This information is never transmitted to us; it stays in the local database described in Section 2.

Calorie figures shown in the App are estimates, calculated on your device from published MET values and the body weight you enter. They are not medical measurements.

The App is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. See the Terms of Use for the full fitness disclaimer.
