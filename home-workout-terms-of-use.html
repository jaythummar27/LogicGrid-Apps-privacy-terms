It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Nothing in the App is medical advice, and it is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.

Before You Start

Talk to a doctor or another qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new exercise routine — particularly if you are pregnant or recently gave birth, are recovering from surgery or an injury, have a heart, joint, back or blood-pressure condition, take medication that affects exercise, or have not exercised in a long time.

While You Train

Work at a level that suits you. Warm up first, follow the form instructions shown for each exercise, and stop straight away if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath. Seek medical attention if symptoms persist. You know your body better than an app does — skip or swap anything that does not feel right.

About the Numbers

Calorie figures shown in the App are estimates, calculated on your device from published MET values and the body weight you enter. They are approximations for comparison over time, not measurements. Workout durations and program structures are general guidance, not a prescription tailored to you by a professional.

About Results

Fitness results depend on many factors including your starting point, consistency, sleep, nutrition, genetics and health. Individual results vary, and the App makes no promise about any specific outcome.

Your Responsibility

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of any information you enter, such as your body weight
