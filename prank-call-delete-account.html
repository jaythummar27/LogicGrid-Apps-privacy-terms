Delete your World Chat account

You can permanently delete the account you use in the Prank Call app. Uninstalling the app only removes data on your device; it does not tell our server to delete your account.

If you still have the app

  1. Open Settings in Prank Call.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and tap Delete permanently.

If you uninstalled the app

Email logicgridapps@gmail.com from the Gmail address linked to your World Chat account. Include your World Chat username and say that you request account deletion. If your account was not linked to Gmail, contact us with the username so we can explain available verification options. Do not send your password, Google ID token, or session token.

Request account deletion by email

We verify ownership before deleting an account requested by email. The in-app option deletes the authenticated account directly.

What is removed

Deletion removes your World Chat account, public profile, sign-in email and account identifier, sessions, messages, conversations, connections, group memberships, and reports associated with your account. Other group members' own accounts and messages remain. Temporary image uploads expire automatically after about 30 minutes. Local custom caller data can be removed from the app or by clearing its device storage.

See our Privacy Policy for details, or email us at logicgridapps@gmail.com.
