Information We Collect

Section 03

We respect your privacy by adhering to a strict data minimization philosophy. We do not operate remote user account servers, and core features function completely offline on your device.

  • Custom DIY Caller Info: Names, phone numbers, custom audio, photos, or video files you optionally choose to import for personalized custom prank callers. This information stays strictly inside your local device storage.

Camera & Audio Processing Transparency

Section 04

During simulated video calls, the App offers an authentic experience by displaying a small front-camera Picture-in-Picture (PiP) self-preview in the corner of your screen.

Real-Time In-Memory Camera Preview Only: When you grant camera permission, your front camera is accessed via Google's official Android CameraX API exclusively to draw a real-time self-view on your display.

Key Safeguards:
• Camera frames are never recorded, captured, encoded, or saved to device storage.
• Camera frames are never transmitted over the internet, streamed to external servers, or shared with third parties.
• The camera hardware is optional (android.hardware.camera required="false"); you may decline camera permission and still use all audio and video call simulations.

Audio Management

The MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS permission is used purely by Android's audio system to toggle sound between the loudspeaker and earpiece during call simulation, and to control the simulated ringtone and voice track volume. The App does NOT record your microphone audio during calls or upload audio recordings.
