Introduction

Section 01

Welcome to Recover Deleted Messages (the "App"), developed by Varshaben Mukeshbhai Thummar ("we," "us," "our"), based in Gujarat, India. The App is a personal utility for Android that saves the content of notifications so you can still read a message after the sender deletes it, and that finds and removes duplicate photos, videos and files to free up storage.

This Privacy Policy describes exactly what information the App reads, why, and where it goes. Please read it carefully before using the App.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall and do not use the App.

This policy covers every feature of the App: notification-based message capture, deletion detection, media saving, duplicate photo/video/file scanning, storage analysis, optional contact de-duplication, and advertising served through Google AdMob.

Information We Collect

Section 02

We collect information in two ways: data the App reads from your device to power its features, and data collected by the third-party SDKs it includes (detailed in Section 9).

2.1 Data Read From Your Device

  • Notification content (if you grant notification access and select an app): message text, sender name, conversation name, group flag, timestamp, source app — see Section 3
  • Photos and videos (if you grant media access): file names, sizes, dates, dimensions, and file content used only to compute local duplicate-detection hashes — see Section 4
  • Contacts (only if you open the duplicate-contacts feature) — see Section 6

All of this is stored exclusively on your device, in the App's private storage (Android Room/SQLite database and private files). We do not operate a server and none of it is transmitted to us.

2.2 Data NOT Collected by Us

We do not collect, store, or process on our own servers: your name, email address, phone number, bank details, passwords, one-time codes, or any account identifier — because the App has no account system and no server. There is no Firebase, no Analytics SDK and no Crash-reporting SDK in this App.

Notification Access & Message Data

Section 03
Google Play Sensitive Permission: This App uses Android's Notification Listener capability, classified as a sensitive permission under Google Play policy. The following disclosure is provided in full transparency and compliance with that policy.

Why We Request It

This is the App's core feature. When a messaging app you have chosen posts a notification, the App reads its content and saves a copy in a local database on your device, so that if the sender later deletes that message, your saved copy remains readable. A disclosure explaining this is shown before the Android system permission screen ever appears.

What We Read

Message text Sender name & conversation name Timestamp & group-chat flag Notification avatar / attached image (only if media saving is on)

What We NEVER Read

Notifications from apps you have not selected Any app's private storage or database Anything on WhatsApp's or any provider's servers Group summaries ("3 new messages") or progress notifications

How It Is Processed

  • Only the apps you explicitly select in Settings are ever read. Everything else is ignored and discarded.
  • Parsing happens 100% on-device. The saved content is stored in a local database.
  • Captured content is never transmitted to any server — no code path in the App reads the message database and sends it anywhere.

This Permission Is Optional

Notification access is optional and can be revoked at any time from Android Settings → Notification access. Without it, message capture simply does not run; the duplicate-photo and storage-cleaner features work independently of it.

Photos, Videos & Media

Section 04

The App requests photo and video access so its duplicate-finder, storage-cleaner and storage-analyser features can scan your media library through Android's MediaStore, and through folders you pick yourself with Android's folder picker.

Key privacy facts about media access:
• Duplicate detection is done by computing a SHA-256 content hash entirely on your device — files are never uploaded
• Media attached to a notification is saved only when the notification genuinely carries it; otherwise the App records it as "media, not saved" rather than pretending to have a copy
• A file is only copied into the App's own storage when you explicitly choose to save that item
• The App does not request "All files access" (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) and cannot browse your whole filesystem

Media access is optional. Message capture works without it; only the duplicate-finder and cleaner features need it.

Device Storage & Cleaner Features

Section 05

The cleanup screens (large files, screenshots, old downloads, empty files, duplicate photos/videos/files) build suggestions from the media index described in Section 4. These are suggestions only — nothing is deleted automatically. You must select items and confirm, and on Android 11 and above the Android system itself shows an additional confirmation dialog before anything is removed or trashed.

Contacts (Optional Feature)

Section 06

If — and only if — you open the duplicate-contacts screen, the App asks to read your contacts to compare entries and show likely duplicates, and to write contacts so you can merge or remove the ones you choose. Contact data is processed on-device and is never uploaded. Declining this permission disables only that one feature.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Section 07
AdMob Disclosure: This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data from your device to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This section explains how advertising works in the App.

Ad Formats Used

Banner ads on the main screens, native ads on a small number of screens, and interstitial (full-screen) ads shown only at natural transition points, paced so they cannot appear more than once every 45 seconds.

Data Collected by AdMob

Google AdMob independently collects the following data for ad serving and measurement purposes:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language)
  • IP address (used to infer approximate geographic location)
  • App interaction signals (ad impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under its own privacy policy. We do not have access to or control over AdMob's data collection, and ad requests never contain your messages, photos, videos, files or contacts.

Ad Personalization

By default, AdMob may serve personalized ads based on your interests and prior app activity. You can opt out at any time:

  • On your Android device: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Or reset your Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID
  • For EU/EEA/UK users: the App implements Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent, and a "Ad privacy choices" option appears in Settings where consent applies

Purchasing the ad-free subscription (Section 8) stops all ad requests. Ads are never shown during a permission request or while a scan or deletion is in progress.

For Google's data practices related to advertising, see: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Privacy FAQ.

Subscriptions & Billing

Section 08

The App offers an optional subscription whose only benefit is removing ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, with entitlements managed through RevenueCat. We never see or handle your card details — Google processes payment directly.

RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription status, and an anonymous app-user identifier, in order to keep the ad-free entitlement working across your devices. It does not receive your messages, photos, videos, files or contacts.
