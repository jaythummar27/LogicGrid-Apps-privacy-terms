Notification-Based Recovery: How It Works & Its Limits

Section 05

Android's notification listener feature allows the App, once you explicitly enable it in system Settings, to read notifications posted on your device. When a messaging app you have selected posts a notification, the App reads its content and stores a copy locally, so deleting the original message elsewhere does not remove that copy.

The App cannot do any of the following, and never claims to:
  • Read WhatsApp's or any other app's private storage or database
  • Decrypt, bypass or weaken any messaging app's encryption
  • Retrieve messages, media or backups from any provider's servers
  • Recover anything that existed before the App was installed and notification access was granted
  • Recover a message for which no notification was ever posted on your device

Conditions That Must All Hold

The App, notification access, and app selection must be active; notifications must not be muted or hidden; and the listener must be connected when the message arrives. Manufacturer battery managers, Android version differences, and changes the messaging apps make to their own notification format are outside our control and may affect what is captured.

🖼️

Media Recovery Limitations

Section 06
  • The App saves an image only when a notification genuinely carries one; such previews are usually lower resolution than the original
  • Where a notification only indicates that media was received, the App records that plainly rather than producing a file
  • For media still present on your device, the App can find and copy it — this is locating an existing file, not undeleting one
  • On Android 11+, items in the system trash can be shown and restored; Android 10 and below has no such trash
  • A file permanently erased from device storage cannot be recovered by this or any non-root app
🧹

Cleaner & Deletion Features

Section 07
  • Every cleanup category is a suggestion. The App never deletes anything on its own initiative
  • Duplicate detection compares file content (SHA-256), not file names — only byte-for-byte identical files are marked as duplicates
  • You must select items and confirm; on Android 11+ the system shows a further confirmation dialog
  • Deletion you confirm is your decision and, in most cases, irreversible — review your selection before confirming
